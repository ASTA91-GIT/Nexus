CONFIDENTIAL REPORT: OPERATION RED DAWN
Date: 14th August 2024
Location: Mumbai Port Trust, Warehouse 42

At approximately 23:00 hours, surveillance cameras captured two individuals, identified as "Vikram Singh" and "Rahul Sharma". They were seen transferring unmarked crates into a black SUV with license plate MH-01-AB-1234. 

Vikram Singh is known to be affiliated with the "Shadow Syndicate", a local smuggling ring. Rahul Sharma was observed making a phone call from the number +91-9876543210 immediately after the transfer. The SUV then departed towards the Eastern Freeway.

Intelligence suggests the crates may contain contraband electronics. The registered owner of the SUV is a shell corporation named "Global Imports Ltd".
